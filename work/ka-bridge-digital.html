Brand identity

KA Bridge Digital helps small business owners get their websites, branding, and customer inquiries working together. The name says it: the company is the bridge between how a business looks online and how it runs day to day. So I built the logo around a bridge, made out of pixels, to bring in the digital side. Typography

Roca is the primary font, used for the logo and headlines.

Questrial is the secondary font for supporting text. It looks best in uppercase.

Color Palette

The idea behind it

Most of KA Bridge Digital's customers are small local businesses, so the brand needed to feel professional without feeling cold. The pixel bridge connects the business and the customer, and the grainy blue background keeps it from feeling flat. Roca gives it weight, and Questrial keeps the supporting text clean and easy to read.
