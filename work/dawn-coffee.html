Case Study — Concept Brand

Dawn Coffee

A branding and seasonal marketing campaign concept for Dawn, a modern coffee shop blending daily ritual with culture and community.

Brand identity

Typography


Beth Ellen Regular is the primary font — warm and handwritten, used for the logo and headlines.

Times New Roman is the complementary font for longer text, especially paragraphs. It's easy to read and keeps everything looking clean and organized.

Marketing campaign — Christmas launch

For the Christmas launch I made a 9-post Instagram grid called "Sip the Season," a weekly "Drink of the Week" email, and tote bags for Dawn's Holiday Acoustic Night. The idea was to give people a few different ways to feel the season, whether they're scrolling, reading an email, or leaving the event with a bag.

Design process

The name came from when people drink coffee. Most of us have it in the morning, right when we wake up. I tied that to a saying I grew up with. The phrase is "the early bird gets the worm," but in Nigeria we change it a little and say the early bird gets the fattest worm. That became the whole idea behind the brand: if you start early and do what you need to do, you get the best of what's out there.

I wanted Dawn to feel homey and warm, not rigid and over-structured, so I went with handwritten script for the type. I wanted the bird to feel just as hand-drawn, so I built the bird and the worm by combining pieces from different sources until they became one drawing that matched the lettering.
