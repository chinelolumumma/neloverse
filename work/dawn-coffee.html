Case Study — Concept Brand

Dawn Coffee

A branding and seasonal marketing campaign concept for Dawn, a modern coffee shop blending daily ritual with culture and community.

  • ClientDawn Coffee (concept)
  • RoleBrand & Marketing Designer
  • Year2025
  • ToolsIllustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

Brand identity

"The early bird gets the worm." The brand identity is built around warmth, minimalism, and daily ritual — the bird mark reflects Dawn's tagline and philosophy.

Mockups

Dawn's branding extended into everyday applications — packaging, signage, and in-store touchpoints that carry the identity into the physical space.

Marketing campaign — Christmas launch

A 9-post Instagram grid launching Dawn's holiday campaign, "Sip the Season," blending product, lifestyle, and seasonal storytelling. A weekly email introduced a "Drink of the Week" alongside seasonal product and event announcements. Branded tote bags were designed as promotional touchpoints for Dawn's Holiday Acoustic Night, serving as a memorable takeaway.

Design process

The process focused on exploring type, word meaning, and color to capture Dawn's identity. The word "dawn" symbolizes new beginnings — a personal connection to growing up hearing "the early bird gets the worm."

Dawn Coffee positions itself as more than a cafe — a cultural hub for community, music, and daily ritual. This campaign demonstrates how branding, social media, and marketing design create a cohesive seasonal experience.
